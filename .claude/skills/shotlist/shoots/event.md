# Shoot: an event

A visiting author gives a talk at the Bok Center, with a Q&A and a reception after. It's run-and-gun: nothing repeats, so the crew has to get the arc of the evening as it happens and the details in between.

## What coverage means here

- **The place, before people.** The outside of the building or the room's entrance, signage or a poster for the event, the empty room set up.
- **Arrivals.** People coming in, finding seats, greeting each other: the beginning of the story.
- **The room at its fullest.** A wide from the back that shows the crowd and the speaker together; how big was it?
- **The speaker, more than one way.** Wide at the podium, medium, close-up, and from the side, with gestures and expression; clean backgrounds without an exit sign over their head.
- **The audience.** Listening, laughing, taking notes, a raised hand: reactions and cutaways, from the front, facing them.
- **The Q&A exchange.** A questioner and the speaker answering, ideally a pair that can cut together.
- **Details.** The book, name cards, the coffee table, hands holding a program: inserts for transitions and for the social post's crop.
- **The end.** Applause, the signing line, the reception, people leaving.
- **Vertical frames.** A few shot portrait for social, since a landscape frame can't always be cropped to 9:16.
- **Releases and signage.** A note of who was photographed up close and whether a "this event is being recorded" sign was posted.
